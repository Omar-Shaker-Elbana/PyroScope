      TRANSPARENCY

      Methodology

      HotSync filters low-confidence detections, then aggregates both sensors onto the same ~2 km grid and UTC day. Activity is measured in unique active grid-cell days, not raw pixel detections. During the overlap, the union of MODIS and VIIRS active cells is the reference activity count; sensor-specific scale factors estimate the union count from MODIS-only or VIIRS-only observations. FRP is aggregated within each cell and 90-minute acquisition-time bin; the maximum bin sum is retained for that cell-day. On matched cell-days with acquisition times within 180 minutes, a robust median ratio converts VIIRS FRP to a MODIS-equivalent scale. Harmonized FRP averages the two estimates where both exist and uses the calibrated sensor estimate where only one exists. FRP is reported as a daily sum of cell-day peak observations (MW), not daily energy. The method assumes both uploaded CSVs are complete archive exports over their date ranges; detections alone cannot prove that a satellite observed a day with zero fires. These are exploratory estimates, not ground truth. The 375 m VIIRS product has different detection characteristics from MODIS, and some platform/product files may not provide FRP.
